# 🛡️ Client Qualification & Risk Detection Matrix

## Client Signal Evaluation Rules

### Positive Signals (+ Points)
- **Payment Method Verified**: Indicates real buyer intent.
- **Client Rating >= 4.8**: High satisfaction history.
- **Total Spend > $10,000**: Proven track record of paying freelancers.
- **Hires Count > 10**: Experienced with remote hiring and management.

### Red Flag Indicators (- Penalty / Skip)
- **Rating < 4.0**: Multiple negative freelancer reviews (bad communication, disputes).
- **Payment Unverified + $0 Spend + Vague Job Title**: Potential spam or scraper account.
- **Keywords**: "unpaid test", "free trial", "cheap", "must work for $3/hr", "contact on Telegram".
