# 📊 Opportunity Scoring Rules & Guidelines

## 0–100 Intelligence Scoring Matrix

| Component | Max Points | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Skill Match** | 30 pts | Match count of Core (n8n, AI, Python, APIs) & Secondary skills |
| **Client Quality** | 25 pts | Rating (0-5), total USD spend, payment verification, hiring frequency |
| **Budget Fit** | 20 pts | Fixed budget >= $300 or Hourly >= $35/hr |
| **Scope & Clarity** | 15 pts | Description length (>300 chars), clear technical deliverables, low competition |
| **Strategic & Risk** | 10 pts | AI automation alignment (+3 pts bonus), zero red flags (unpaid, cheap, test job) |

## Priority Tiers
- **85–100**: High Priority (Instant Slack notification & auto-proposal generation)
- **70–84**: Worth Reviewing (Generate proposal draft, queue for human review)
- **50–69**: Low Priority (Log to Airtable CRM only)
- **Below 50**: Ignore / Skip (Filter out completely)
